# Network and IT Support Engineer
Island Connect BPO
Cebu, Central Visayas

Information Technology Support
Full time

₱35,000 – ₱45,000 per month

## About the role

We are seeking a Network and IT Support Engineer to keep office and remote
technology reliable. The role combines network administration, user support,
asset management, and security.

## Duties and responsibilities

- Configure and troubleshoot LAN, Wi-Fi, routers, switches, VPN, and firewalls.
- Provide technical support for laptops, accounts, software, and email.
- Manage user access, passwords, endpoint security, and device inventory.
- Monitor network performance and resolve connectivity incidents.
- Document tickets, configurations, procedures, and knowledge-base articles.
- Assist with backups, system updates, and disaster recovery tests.
- Coordinate with developers and cloud administrators during deployments.

## Qualifications

- Degree or equivalent experience in Computer Engineering, Information
  Technology, or a related discipline.
- Familiarity with Cisco Packet Tracer, TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, and networking.
- Strong troubleshooting, customer service, and documentation skills.
- Experience with Linux, cloud services, scripting, or automation is a plus.
